Logo concepts

Three directions built on Veyra's system: navy #102A43 · teal #0E7490 / #2BB3A6 · IBM Plex. Each concept ships as icon, horizontal (light + dark), vertical, wordmark, and mono variants.

Concept 01

Vertex — the verified pivot

An evolution of the current favicon: a white V with a gap at its valley where a teal node sits — the point of inspection. The navy tile gets true depth via a subtle diagonal gradient. Reads instantly as "checked", keeps full continuity with what's already in the product. Works as tiny as 16px.

Concept 02

Receipt — value you can audit

The V told as a lab receipt: the left arm is the solid observed value; the right arm is the backing data, dashed like report lines; the teal node at the vertex is the insight — the one point that was actually inspected. The wordmark echoes it with a dashed baseline rule.

Concept 03

Reference Interval — typography-led

The mark is a lab reference-interval bar: end ticks, a teal in-range segment, and a punched-out value dot sitting on the rule. The same interval bar anchors the wordmark, with a spaced mono "HEALTH". The most literal, product-true direction, and the most typographic.
